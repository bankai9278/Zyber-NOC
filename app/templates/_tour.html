How Zyber NOC works

  1. This is sample data

    You are looking at the real app in read-only demo mode. The eight devices and 90 days of checks are made up, on documentation addresses. Nothing here pings a real device.

  2. One check, saved for good

    On a local PC, each check runs Windows ping five times against one saved device. The result goes into SQLite, and uptime, incidents, and the status bars are worked out only from those saved rows.

  3. Where to look

    The Dashboard has counts and recent incidents. Open a device for its 90-day bar and latency chart. Topology shows why the floor 2 access point is unreachable: its parent switch is down.
